# Smart IoT Inventory Management

## Overview
This project is an IoT-based solution designed to automate product inventory tracking using camera vision and weight sensors. The system monitors product quantities and expiration dates in real time, helping users manage supplies efficiently.

## Key Features

- QR Code Scanning: Uses a camera to scan QR codes and identify products and their expiration dates.
- Weight Monitoring: Uses weight sensors to track product quantities and detect low inventory levels.
- Expiration Tracking: Monitors expiration dates and identifies products approaching expiration.
- Automated Shopping List: Automatically adds products to a shopping list when stock is low or expiration dates are approaching.
- Waste Reduction: Helps prevent product waste and ensures timely replenishment.

## How It Works

1. The camera scans a product's QR code to identify it and retrieve its expiration date.
2. Weight sensors continuously monitor the available product quantity.
3. The system evaluates inventory levels and expiration dates.
4. Products running low or approaching expiration are automatically added to the shopping list.

## Project Goals
- Automate inventory management.
- Improve resource efficiency.
- Reduce food and product waste.
- Simplify everyday shopping and replenishment.
